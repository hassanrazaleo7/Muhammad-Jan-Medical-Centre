✚ Your safety matters
!

Some symptoms need medical care first.

Knowing when to seek urgent help is part of responsible care.

Please don’t wait for a routine appointment

Seek urgent medical help if you have:

  • ●Sudden, severe, or rapidly worsening abdominal pain
  • ●Blood in vomit or black, bloody stools
  • ●Blood in your urine or inability to pass urine
  • ●Fever with painful urination, side or back pain, or vomiting
  • ●Difficulty breathing, chest pain, fainting, or confusion
  • ●Any symptom that feels like an emergency
In an emergency: contact your local emergency service or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not use a website enquiry form for urgent symptoms.

This information is general and does not diagnose a condition. If you are worried, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
